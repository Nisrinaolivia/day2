New PS s 8.2
The wording of s 8.2 of the new PS (prohibiting use of information obtained in mediation for personal gain) is wider than
that used in s 5.4 of the old PS (prohibiting use of information “about participants”) and is to be preferred. New PS s 8.3
Many mediators are members of a primary profession, eg they are lawyers, social workers, counsellors, engineers and
so on. Section 8.3 (and s 5.7 of the old PS) means, for example, that a lawyer mediator must comply with the solicitors'
or barristers' rules of professional conduct, as relevant. It suggests that those professional conduct rules are to prevail in
the event of conflict with the PS (how else can the lawyer “adhere” to them) but it would be preferable if s 8.3 made this
explicit. 43
New PS s 8.4
Section 8.4 of the new PS provides a rare mention of the interests of other stakeholders. Some commentators argue that
more weight should be given to the interests of persons who are not parties to the mediation but are nonetheless affected by
its outcome. 44 There is no consensus on this matter at the current time, although some insights can be gained from relevant
literature. 45
