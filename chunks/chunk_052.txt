New PS s 12
The new provision (s 12) does nothing to further the cause in having mediation accepted as a profession devoted to public
service. In particular, the following points have been lost from s 13 of the old PS:
1. The old standards seemed more concerned with the profession's relationship with the public, with multiple
references to educating the public about mediation and representing mediation accurately and objectively so
that the public could make informed choices about dispute resolution processes (see eg ss 13.1, 13.3, 13.4). 2. Importantly, the only reference in the PS to “empowerment” of the participants through mediation (see s
13.1(b) of the old PS) has been omitted. 3.
