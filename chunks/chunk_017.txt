Commentary: s 3 Conducting mediation: Preliminary conference or intake
New PS s 3.1
Section 3.1(a) of the new PS takes up most of the statement of principle from s 3 of the old PS: mediators have to give the
participants a description of the mediation process and the steps involved. No guidance is given as to how detailed this
information should be, so this decision will be left to individual mediators. Under s 3.1(b) of the new PS, mediators also have to advise participants about how to make a formal complaint “in relation
to the mediator”. It might be prudent to expand this provision so that participants are also made aware of how to make a
complaint in relation to the relevant mediation organisation or body (eg Relationships Australia or the AAT). New PS s 3.2
Section 3.2 of the new PS is in similar terms to, and covers all major issues from, s 3.3 of the old PS. It sets out the
objectives of, and a list of matters to be undertaken in, a preliminary conference or intake session. However, as discussed
below, many provisions from s 3 of the old PS have been omitted. Under the old PS, mediators were required to inform the participants of how to access the Standards (see s 3.6 of the
old PS). Under the new PS, participants must be advised “about the NMAS and how it can be accessed” (s 3.2(d)).
