13 Waiver by MSB
In exceptional circumstances the MSB may, conditionally or otherwise, waive compliance with any provision of the
Practice Standards on application by a RMAB. There does not appear to be an equivalent provision in the old PS. Potential shortcomings of the new PS
The new PS are relatively brief. They are reasonably well-structured. People who are familiar with mediation will find the
PS easy to read. They cover parts of the mediation process that previously had been glossed over, such as intake sessions and
preliminary conferences. However, the very brevity and simplicity of the new PS is also the source of their weakest points. Long
portions of information (all of those sections underlined in the table above) have been deleted from the old PS. As a consequence,

The mediation community needs to consider how the information lost from the old PS can best be disseminated to various
stakeholders. Anyone (mediators, lawyers, court and tribunal officials) who is in a position of having to advise potential
participants about what to expect in mediation might consider collating the material deleted from the old PS into a supplementary
user guide for distribution to relevant participants.
