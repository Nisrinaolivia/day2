Section 7.4 of the new PS is one of the most important provisions in the Standards. 35 It replaces s 9.1 of the old PS. The
old and new provisions (see s 9.1 and s 7.4 respectively) are almost identical except that “will” (old PS) has become a
“must” (new PS). This provision is now prescriptive in its format. It provides: “A mediator must support participants to
reach agreements freely, voluntarily, without undue influence and on the basis of informed consent”. Unfortunately, none
of the important terms in this provision are defined in either set of Standards. As mentioned earlier, the concept of self-
determination is not explained fully either. Resort might be had to the work of scholars such as Welch and Nolan-Haley to
fill the gaps. The essential elements of self-determination are: 36
• Active and direct participation by the parties in the process of communication and negotiation. • Informed consent on the part of the parties as to both process and outcome.
