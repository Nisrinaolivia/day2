7 Competence
Statement of principle: “Mediators must be competent and have relevant skills and knowledge”. This wording has been
absorbed into ss 8.1 and 10.1 of the new PS. As to the provisions in ss 7.1-7.2, see s 8.8 of the new PS. 7.3: “Mediators should be competent and have the capacity to apply knowledge, skills and an ethical understanding
and commitment in the areas listed below. Mediators demonstrate competence by showing that they have the requisite
knowledge and skills and can apply them”. The section goes on to say that mediators are required to ensure “ongoing
professional development is focused on achieving and maintaining competencies including (a) KNOWLEDGE in areas
including, but not limited to … (b) SKILLS, including, but not limited to: … and (c) ETHICAL UNDERSTANDINGS in
relation to …”The underlined portions have been OMITTED from s 7.3 of the old PS.Three separate lists were then given,
dealing in turn with knowledge, skills, and ethical understandings. As mentioned above, there have been some minor
changes in the lists of knowledge and skills. Section 7.3(c) provided that mediators should have an ethical understanding in
relation to:
(i) The avoidance of conflicts of interest. (ii) Marketing and advertising of mediation. (iii) Confidentiality, privacy and reporting obligations. (iv) Neutrality and impartiality. (v) Fiduciary obligations. (vi) Supporting fairness and equity in mediation.
