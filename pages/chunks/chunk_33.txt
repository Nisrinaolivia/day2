Section 7.3 of the new PS is similar to s 5.1 of the old PS, setting out the circumstances in which a mediator can continue
to mediate, despite a bias or conflict, or a potential for one. At least, that seems to be the intention of the section. However,
it currently only addresses situations involving a conflict of interest. It should be expanded to also cover situations
involving a bias. At present, s 7.2 does not cover actual grounds of bias or conflict, only potential ones; s 7.3 does not deal with situations of
bias, only with a conflict of interest. Neither section allows for perceptions of conflict or bias. New PS s 7.4
Section 7.4 of the new PS is one of the most important provisions in the Standards. 35 It replaces s 9.1 of the old PS. The
old and new provisions (see s 9.1 and s 7.4 respectively) are almost identical except that “will” (old PS) has become a
“must” (new PS).
