Many mediators will be trained to deal with these matters as part of their opening statement and they will do so
instinctively. However, this may not be the case with new mediators (or for some “old” mediators). The new PS do not have a provision equivalent to s 3.5 of the old PS. Section 3.5 encouraged the participants to reduce
their agreement to mediate and their agreement regarding confidentiality (or its waiver) to writing. This is a matter of
good practice and it is difficult to understand why the provision has been omitted. It is not enough simply to explain to
participants the nature and content of such agreements should they happen to have entered into them (see s 3.2(b) of the
new PS).
