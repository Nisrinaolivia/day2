Commentary: s 7 Procedural fairness and impartiality
New PS s 7.1
Mediators have long had a duty to conduct mediation in an impartial manner. 33 To meet this requirement, mediators
generally introduce themselves in an equal manner, perform equal non-verbals such as handshaking, address the parties in
a similar manner, sit an equal distance from each party, maintain equal eye contact, give each of the parties equal air time
and so on. If a mediator becomes aware that he or she is starting to dislike a party and/or that party's attitudes, to the extent
that it is interfering with the mediator's conduct of the mediation, most mediators would not continue with the mediation.
