New PS s 11
Section 11 of the new PS picks up three points from s 12 of the old PS, but omits the three additional points mentioned
above. Section 11:
• Does not appear to require mediators to explain how their fees are calculated. • Does not require the agreement in relation to fees to be in writing (to be fair, neither did s 12.2 of the old PS, but
mention of “any written agreement” including a description of any fee arrangement might have encouraged all
concerned to record their agreement in writing). • Might discourage mediators from offering their services on a pro bono basis or from mediating with organisations
such as the Legal Aid Office and community justice programs where mediators are paid at minimal rates. If a
mediator is conducting a mediation on a pro bono basis, he or she might well set time constraints on the mediation
which would not apply if the mediator was conducting the mediation at their normal rate of remuneration. Section
11.2 should be amended along the lines of the wording used in s 12.3 of the old PS. New PS
12 Provision of information and promotion of services
12.1: “A mediator must accurately represent their qualifications and experience and how they conduct their mediations”. 12.2: “A mediator must not guarantee results or outcomes from the mediation process or make statements likely to create
false expectations about favourable results”. Regard should also be had to s 10.1(c)(viii) which requires honesty in marketing and advertising. Also see s 12.3 (any information used for evaluation purposes must be de-identified) and s 12.4 (a mediator qualified under
this scheme can describe themselves as “Accredited Mediator NMAS”).
