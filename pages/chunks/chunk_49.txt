This commentary focuses on the ethical aspects of mediation practice. Section 10.1 states that mediators must have
“an understanding of the ethical principles” outlined in that section. Section 7.3 of the old PS used slightly different
terminology, requiring mediators to have “an ethical understanding and commitment” in the listed areas. Although the
difference may be slight, ideally mediators should understand and commit to the various ethical principles. Section 7.3 of the old PS emphasised that the lists of required competences were not exhaustive by using the terms
“including” and “including, but not limited to”. While the last phrase sounds like legalese, it should be imported into
the new PS.
