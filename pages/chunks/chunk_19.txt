New PS s 3.2
Section 3.2 of the new PS is in similar terms to, and covers all major issues from, s 3.3 of the old PS. It sets out the
objectives of, and a list of matters to be undertaken in, a preliminary conference or intake session. However, as discussed
below, many provisions from s 3 of the old PS have been omitted. Under the old PS, mediators were required to inform the participants of how to access the Standards (see s 3.6 of the
old PS). Under the new PS, participants must be advised “about the NMAS and how it can be accessed” (s 3.2(d)). It
appears that it is then left up to the participants to find their way to the Standards. The provision would benefit from a clear
statement instructing mediators to provide participants with a copy of the Standards. In this respect, the old PS is better –
participants must be helped to get access to these Standards (compare s 3.2(d) of the new PS with s 3.6 of the old PS). Other provisions omitted from old PS s 3
